# Code of Conduct

Rustbound Idle follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be welcoming, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact links at
<https://mnix.dev/#social>. Do not open a public issue for a private conduct report.

The maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy as far as possible, and may
remove content, restrict participation, or ban a contributor when needed. Good-faith reports will not
result in retaliation.

This policy applies in the repository, Discussions, project meetings, and other spaces where someone is
representing Rustbound Idle.
